## Design Context

### Users
Fashion shoppers browsing THELIVINGSTOP on phones, tablets, and desktop. They are looking for a confident, distinctive wardrobe piece, need to inspect product details quickly, and expect a calm, trustworthy path from product discovery to checkout.

### Brand Personality
Refined, expressive, intentional. The experience should feel premium and editorial while staying direct and easy to shop.

### Aesthetic Direction
Luxury fashion storefront with an editorial composition: warm off-white canvas, near-black type, signal red accents, strong product photography, generous whitespace, and controlled motion. The interface should feel composed and expensive rather than glossy or overly decorated.

### Design Principles
- Make the product and its material the visual focus.
- Preserve the same shopping information architecture across phone, tablet, and desktop.
- Use motion to orient and confirm, with one memorable signature SVG moment and restrained micro-interactions.
- Keep touch targets generous, layouts fluid, and fallbacks functional without JavaScript.
- Treat loading, errors, focus states, and reduced-motion preferences as part of the luxury experience.
